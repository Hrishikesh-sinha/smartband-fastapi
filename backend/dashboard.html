Privacy controls

  • Data minimisation: the band sends only pulse estimate and MQ135 value. No name, location or raw signal.
  • Pseudonymous ID: readings are linked to smartband-01, not to a person.
  • Secret protection: device keys are kept outside the readings database and never sent over the network.
  • Restricted access: every data request needs a valid token; failures are logged.
  • Integrity: HMAC-SHA-256 on every message, sequence numbers against replay.
